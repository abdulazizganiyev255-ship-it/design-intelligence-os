# taste-critique — Design Intelligence OS v0.3

**Stage:** S04 and S07. **Mode:** supervised ChatGPT role workflow; not an autonomous agent.

## Objective
Critique visual character, composition and generic-AI patterns against task and references without hallucinated scoring.

## Inputs
approved low-fi wireframe, two actual visual candidates or screenshots, owner taste/rejections, source-based reference cards.

## Procedure
1. Start with intended emotional/brand positioning and primary visual reading order.
2. Compare structural signature, balance, rhythm, typography contrast, density, surfaces and meaningful accent use.
3. Flag generic repeated KPI cards, arbitrary gradients, glassmorphism, decorative 3D or color-only differentiation **only when they harm the task or identity**.
4. Perform direct A/B critique: named strongest element, weakest element, regression risk and distinctive pattern of each.
5. Use critical reviewer to challenge overconfident 'premium' claims; request side-by-side rendered proof and owner selection.

## Outputs
Taste review card: evidence, originality/clarity tradeoff, preserve/fix, comparative recommendation and Owner decision pending.

## Acceptance / Quality gate
At least two real, materially different directions; no numeric S+ without complete rubric, no UI copy/metrics invented, no web-only skill treated as native QA.
