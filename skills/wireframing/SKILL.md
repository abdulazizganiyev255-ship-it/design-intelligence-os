# wireframing — Design Intelligence OS v0.3

**Stage:** S03. **Mode:** supervised ChatGPT role workflow; not an autonomous agent.

## Objective
Translate approved navigation and real content into low fidelity hierarchy and primary-action wireframes.

## Inputs
S02 screen map/flows, task priorities, representative long strings/data, 320px constraints.

## Procedure
1. Sketch grayscale containers and reading order without colors, shadows or effects.
2. Try at least two grouping/hierarchy options if primary task remains ambiguous.
3. Expose main CTA, label, confirmation, errors and empty state; annotate content sources.
4. Review small viewport and long Uzbek money/date labels; check scroll and safe area.
5. Only after Board acceptance hand off wireframe to art-direction; do not polish buttons.

## Outputs
wireframes with annotations, hierarchy rationale and next-gate risks.

## Acceptance / Quality gate
Primary task explainable without color, actual copy present, all critical states covered and 320px constraints acknowledged.
