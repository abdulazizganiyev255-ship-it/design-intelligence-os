## Claim
Design principle or defect in one clear sentence.

## Observed evidence
E-IDs + source asset/capture, before/after or test log. Separate observation from UX hypothesis and aesthetic judgment.

## When it applies
Task, device, content, data truth, implementation constraints.

## When it fails or is unsuitable
Explicit counterexample and observed regression; don't apply design rules universally.

## Result
What was tested, by whom, outcome, non-results and unknowns.

## Reuse instructions
Which stage/skill can retrieve it; exact pass/fail check; adapt to platform and verified content.

## Promotion gate
Human-repeated/verified outcome + provenance + accepted license + documented context. Until then do NOT label this VALIDATED_PATTERN or add as authoritative RAG content.
